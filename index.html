About

So what is H2M Labs?

Honestly? It's one product manager who got tired of software that tries to do everything, and started building the simpler version instead.

Every product begins the same way — a problem I kept bumping into, no tool I actually liked, so I spec'd one out and shipped it. No committees, no features nobody asked for. Just focused tools that do one job well.

Everything ships under a single name — H2M Labs — so when you spot it on a statement, you know exactly who built the thing and where to find them.
